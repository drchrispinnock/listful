**Description** [4000]:

Listful is a simple outliner for task lists. Each list is a plain Markdown file in iCloud Drive, so your lists are on all your devices, open in any text editor, and never locked in. There's no account and no subscription.

OUTLINE YOUR TASKS
• Nest tasks under tasks, as deep as you like, and fold them away
• Split a list into sections with headings
• Indent, outdent and reorder from the bar above the keyboard, or drag to move a task with its subtasks
• Return starts the next task, so you can type a list straight in

TICK THINGS OFF
• Tap a circle to mark a task done; swipe for done or delete
• Decruft hides finished tasks (or deletes them) to keep a list tidy
• Sort a list A to Z

REPEATING CHECKLISTS
• Daily, weekly, monthly or yearly checklists (set up in Listful for Mac) get a fresh copy each time, carrying over what you didn't finish

YOUR FILES, EVERYWHERE
• Lists live in iCloud Drive › Listful, as Markdown that any app can read
• Folders keep work and home apart
• Changes made on your Mac or iPad appear here within seconds
• Prefer Dropbox or another folder? Choose it in Settings

Listful for Mac uses the same folder and is free to download from github.com/drchrispinnock/listful.

**Review notes:**
Listful needs no account. It keeps task lists as Markdown files in its iCloud Drive folder (iCloud Drive › Listful), so sign in to iCloud with iCloud Drive on to try it; the first launch shows an empty sidebar, and + makes a list. Settings › Choose Another Folder lets you use any other folder instead. A Mac version exists (distributed outside the App Store) but isn't needed.
