# Listful privacy policy

*Last updated: 17 November 2026*

Listful is a task list app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It doesn't collect
any information about you.

- **Your lists stay yours.** Listful keeps each task list as a Markdown
  file in a folder you control: normally iCloud Drive › Listful, or another
  folder you choose. They're stored and synced by iCloud Drive (or by the
  service that folder belongs to) under your own account. Listful's
  developer never sees them.
- **No accounts, analytics, tracking or advertising.** Listful has no
  servers of its own and sends nothing to its developer or to anyone else.
- **Settings** such as whether to show done tasks are kept on your device
  only.
- **Updates (Mac).** The Mac app checks GitHub for new versions. That
  request includes the app's version and macOS version, as any download
  does, but nothing about you or your lists.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new
date.
